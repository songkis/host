GoldenGoose Quickstart Guide

Authentication, Rate Limits, and Integration Protocols

1. Authentication

All REST and WebSocket endpoints require an HMAC API Bearer token in the Authorization: Bearer <API_KEY> header.

2. Signal Frequencies & Regimes

Signals are computed over 3-minute continuous sessions (09:03 to 15:15 KST). Intraday market regimes are classified into BULL, RANGE_NEUTRAL, and BEAR hourly, automatically adjusting stop-loss widths and VWAP −kσ entry sensitivity.

3. Zero-Overnight Policy

GoldenGoose enforces strict intraday time cuts at 15:15 KST to eliminate gap risk. All positions are closed prior to market settlement.

4. Rate Limits & SLA

Standard Tier allows up to 1,200 requests/minute on REST and unlimited streaming messages on dedicated WebSocket multiplexes with sub-50ms latency.
